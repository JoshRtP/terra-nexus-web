# Low Carbon Energy & Biofuels — Approved Topic Page Brief

## Page purpose

Position Terra Nexus as the firm that helps biofuel producers and commodity
traders convert agricultural feedstock carbon-intensity opportunities into
commercially viable, operationally executable, and verification-ready programs.

This is an **Area of Expertise**, not a separate service family. The page must
connect the topic to the existing Terra Nexus service families, audience
segments, engagement models, and any relevant approved proof. A proof module is
optional and is not required for page development or publication.

## Approved audience priority

Biofuel producers and commodity traders/originators are equal primary audiences.

### Biofuel producers

The page should address decisions involving:

- feedstock and facility carbon-intensity economics;
- sourcing regions, crops, practices, and supply scenarios;
- FD-CIC and GREET pathway analysis;
- traceability, recordkeeping, and internal controls;
- partner, technology, and managed-service requirements;
- tax, transaction, and verification readiness.

### Commodity traders and originators

The page should address decisions involving:

- reduced-CI feedstock product and market strategy;
- producer enrollment and field-level evidence;
- aggregation, mass balance, and eligible-volume controls;
- premiums, value sharing, and contractual representations;
- incomplete, untraceable, or non-verifiable supply;
- customer, auditor, and verifier documentation.

Agricultural producers, investors, program partners, technology providers, and
verification bodies are supporting audiences rather than equal primary buyers.

## Geographic and market focus

The page is U.S.-led and should emphasize the connection among agricultural
feedstocks, USDA FD-CIC, GREET, Section 45Z, commercial sourcing, chain of
custody, and verification readiness.

Terra Nexus's capabilities should not be divided into separate service offers by
fuel type. The shared focus is the agricultural feedstock and carbon-intensity
operating challenge across eligible fuel pathways.

## Central market thesis

Low-carbon fuel value is not created by a carbon-intensity calculation alone.
Durable value depends on connecting:

1. field-level agricultural evidence;
2. feedstock carbon-intensity calculations;
3. origination, aggregation, and physical movement;
4. chain-of-custody and attribute allocation;
5. fuel pathway and facility economics;
6. contracts and value sharing;
7. recurring operating controls;
8. audit, tax-diligence, and verification requirements.

A favorable CI score becomes commercially useful only when the underlying
feedstock, evidence, calculations, contracts, controls, and claims can operate
together and withstand independent review.

## Approved differentiation

Terra Nexus combines:

- agricultural and field-level data;
- commodity origination and supply-chain knowledge;
- lifecycle carbon accounting;
- commercial and financial modeling;
- program design and managed operations;
- transaction and investor diligence;
- fraud and non-verifiable-claim assessment;
- audit and verification readiness.

The public positioning should present Terra Nexus as the integration layer among
agronomy, lifecycle modeling, commodity operations, commercial strategy, tax and
legal advisers, technology providers, and accredited verifiers. Terra Nexus
should not be positioned as a substitute for those specialist roles.

## Client journey

The page should show that Terra Nexus can meet clients at different stages:

### Evaluate

Identify addressable CI opportunities, estimate value, compare pathways, and
test the business case.

### Assess

Review sourcing, data, calculations, field evidence, traceability, fraud risks,
non-verifiable claims, and operational readiness.

### Design

Define the operating model, producer and originator program, evidence and
chain-of-custody requirements, contracts, value sharing, and risk allocation.

### Build

Develop tools, controls, workflows, ledgers, exception management, and partner
coordination.

### Launch

Support onboarding, evidence collection, calculation workflows, initial
transactions, and pilot testing.

### Scale

Expand across producers, delivery locations, facilities, crops, and geographies
while maintaining control quality.

### Prepare for verification

Organize evidence, test calculations and claims, perform readiness reviews,
coordinate verifier questions, and resolve gaps before formal assurance or tax
diligence.

## FD-CIC – Carbon Intensity Analysis Platform

Terra Nexus may publicly describe the **FD-CIC – Carbon Intensity Analysis
Platform** as a productized capability supported by proprietary accelerators and
tools.

Approved capability themes include:

- field- and management-unit-level analysis;
- direct use and integration of USDA FD-CIC inputs and outputs;
- scenario comparison across location, yield, nutrients, and practices;
- connection to applicable GREET pathway analysis;
- facility and feedstock value modeling;
- partial-traceability and mixed-supply scenarios;
- value-allocation modeling;
- source-data, unit, assumption, and version tracking;
- reproducible outputs and audit-ready workpapers.

The platform must not be described as replacing USDA FD-CIC, DOE 45ZCF-GREET,
tax advice, certification, or independent verification.

## Engagement models

### Advise

Terra Nexus evaluates opportunities, models economics, assesses pathways,
performs diligence, and designs operating models.

### Manage

Terra Nexus embeds within client-owned programs to operationalize and manage
recurring work, including:

- calculation workflows;
- program and attribute ledgers;
- producer and field-evidence review;
- eligible-volume tracking;
- value allocation;
- exception management;
- audit and verification packages;
- verifier coordination;
- recurring program reporting.

### Operate boundary

Operate is reserved for programs Terra Nexus owns and runs end-to-end under the
Terra Nexus name. It is not part of the public offer on this expertise page.

- ISCC;
- RED II and RED III;

- ISO 22095;

Specific client names or programs are not required for this experience statement.
