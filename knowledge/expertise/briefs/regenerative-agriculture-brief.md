## Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Regenerative Agriculture encompasses farming and land management practices
that aim to restore soil health, increase biodiversity, improve water cycles,
sequester carbon, and build long-term agricultural resilience — while
maintaining or improving farm productivity and profitability.

## Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Regenerative agriculture sits at the intersection of food security, climate
mitigation, and ecosystem health. Pressure from corporate Scope 3 targets,
voluntary carbon markets, consumer-facing sustainability claims, and emerging
regulatory disclosure requirements is driving food companies and commodity
traders to engage with their supply base on production practices.

- Corporate Scope 3 reduction programs sourcing from regenerative farms

- Biodiversity and ecosystem service payments alongside carbon programs
