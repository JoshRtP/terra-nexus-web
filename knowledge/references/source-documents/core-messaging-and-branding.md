# Current Branding and Taglines

Terra Nexus

Simon Sinek - Why-to-What

Why We Exist: To develop a more resilient, sustainable, & prosperous food system by accelerating the adoption of proven solutions that create impact at the intersection of food & climate

How We Achieve that Goal: by working with food & agribusinesses at every node in the value chain to enhance transparency, facilitate collaboration and establish the market mechanisms that unlock shared value across the entire food system

What We Do: is provide the insights, tools, and management expertise to deliver the right solution, in the right place, with the right partners, to capture the largest sources of value for commercial sustainability and climate programs

Other Narrative Types:

What-to-Why:

Terra Nexus is an innovation and insight studio that equips food and agribusinesses with the strategies, tools and supporting capabilities to launch commercial sustainability offerings. We integrate proven solutions that enhance transparency, facilitate collaboration, and reward performance at every node in the food value chain, because we believe that with the right partners, we can build a more resilient, sustainable and prosperous food system
