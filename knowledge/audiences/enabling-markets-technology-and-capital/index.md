# Audience Segments

* [Environmental Markets & Ecosystem Services](environmental-markets-and-ecosystem-services.md) - Project developers, ecosystem service program operators, standards bodies, registries, verifiers, credit buyers, marketplaces, ratings providers, and other market infrastructure organizations that turn environmental outcomes into credible assets, claims, and incentives.
* [Enabling Tech & Solution Providers](enabling-tech-and-solution-providers.md) - MRV platforms, remote sensing providers, farm data systems, traceability tools, lifecycle assessment platforms, soil data companies, supplier engagement platforms, and sustainability software providers that make environmental performance measurable, reportable, and actionable.
* [Private Equity, Venture Capital & Impact Investors](private-equity-venture-capital-and-impact-investors.md) - Capital providers, strategic investors, project finance partners, infrastructure investors, asset managers, institutional asset owners, sovereign investors, and impact funds that invest in food, agriculture, climate, nature, circular economy, and environmental market solutions.
