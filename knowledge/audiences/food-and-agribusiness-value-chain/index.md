# Food & Agribusiness Value Chain

![Food and agribusiness value chain](/references/images/food-agribusiness-value-chain.png)

# Audience Segments

* [Inputs Companies](inputs-companies.md) - Seed, fertilizer, crop protection, biologicals, equipment, animal health, and agronomy solution providers.
* [Agricultural Producers & Integrated Protein Companies](agricultural-producers-and-integrated-protein-companies.md) - Large-scale crop producers, livestock and poultry companies, dairy cooperatives, palm producers, and vertically integrated food production systems.
* [Commodity Traders](commodity-traders.md) - Originators, traders, handlers, processors, exporters, and supply-chain intermediaries that move agricultural commodities from producers to downstream markets.
* [Ingredient & Feed Processors](ingredient-and-feed-processors.md) - Companies converting crops, grains, oils, proteins, and byproducts into food ingredients, feed ingredients, and animal nutrition products.
* [Energy & Biofuels Refiners](energy-and-biofuels-refiners.md) - Ethanol, renewable diesel, biodiesel, SAF, RNG, and low-carbon fuel producers.
* [Food & Beverage Companies](food-and-beverage-companies.md) - Branded food, beverage, alcohol, snack, dairy, packaged food, and food manufacturing companies.
* [Food Retail & Distribution](food-retail-and-distribution.md) - Grocery retailers, club stores, foodservice distributors, wholesalers, and institutional foodservice operators.
* [Food Waste Prevention, Diversion & Recovery](food-waste-prevention-diversion-and-recovery.md) - Organizations focused on food waste prevention, surplus food recovery, donation, composting, organics recycling, anaerobic digestion, and circular food systems.
