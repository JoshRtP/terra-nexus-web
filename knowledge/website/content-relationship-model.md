# Core Relationship

| Content Type | Answers |
|---|---|
| Area of Expertise | What market or food-and-climate topic creates the need to act? |
| Service Line | Which client function owns the decision, and how can Terra Nexus help? |
| Audience | Which value-chain or enabling-market participant is facing the decision? |
| Advise / Manage / Operate | What role does Terra Nexus play? |
| Case Study / Qualification | What evidence shows Terra Nexus can perform the work? |

# Linking Rules

* Every topic page should link to relevant services, audiences, case studies, and insights.
* Every service page should identify primary decision owners and link to relevant topics and proof.
* Every audience page should link to the topics and service lines most relevant to that group.
* Every case study should carry metadata for service, topic, audience, engagement model, geography, commodity, standards, and outcome when available.
* Do not force a one-to-one relationship. The architecture is intentionally many-to-many.
