# Purpose

Use this template for an Area of Expertise page. Topic pages are the primary market-facing calls to action.

# Required Sections

1. **Topic Overview** - What the topic is within Terra Nexus's food-and-climate scope.
2. **Market Context / Why Now** - Current forces creating action. Requires current sources.
3. **Who Is Affected** - Relevant value-chain and enabling-market audiences.
4. **Decisions Clients Face** - Decisions organized by client function.
5. **How Terra Nexus Helps** - Relevant service lines; do not replace them with generic activities.
6. **How We May Engage** - Advise, Manage, and/or Operate.
7. **Relevant Standards, Programs, Tools, or Market Mechanisms** - Only approved and sourced examples.
8. **Case Studies and Qualifications** - Approved proof modules.
9. **Related Insights** - Current articles and explainers.
10. **Call to Action** - A specific next step connected to the market opportunity.
