# Why We Exist

To develop a more resilient, sustainable, & prosperous food system by accelerating the adoption of proven solutions that create impact at the intersection of food & climate.[^core-branding]

# How We Achieve That Goal

By working with food & agribusinesses at every node in the value chain to enhance transparency, facilitate collaboration and establish the market mechanisms that unlock shared value across the entire food system.[^core-branding]

# What We Do

Provide the insights, tools, and management expertise to deliver the right solution, in the right place, with the right partners, to capture the largest sources of value for commercial sustainability and climate programs.[^core-branding]
