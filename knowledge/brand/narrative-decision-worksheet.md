## Option 1 — Simon Sinek Why-to-What (Long)

**Source text:**

> Why We Exist: To develop a more resilient, sustainable, & prosperous food
> system by accelerating the adoption of proven solutions that create impact
> at the intersection of food & climate
>
> How We Achieve that Goal: by working with food & agribusinesses at every
> node in the value chain to enhance transparency, facilitate collaboration
> and establish the market mechanisms that unlock shared value across the
> entire food system
>
> What We Do: is provide the insights, tools, and management expertise to
> deliver the right solution, in the right place, with the right partners,
> to capture the largest sources of value for commercial sustainability and
> climate programs

| Attribute | Assessment [agent-draft] |
|---|---|
| **Intended use** | Structured narrative for investor decks, long-form About pages, or brand manifesto sections |
| **Strengths** | Establishes purpose before services; resonates with mission-aligned audiences; scaffolds the entire value proposition |
| **Weaknesses** | Three-part structure is long for a homepage hero; requires audience familiarity with the Why-How-What model |
| **Length** | Long (three paragraphs) |
| **Primary audience** | Mission-aligned clients, investors, strategic partners who respond to purpose-first framing |
| **Likely placement** | About page or brand manifesto; not recommended as a standalone hero unit |
| **Unresolved choices** | Is the Why-to-What order the preferred sequence for external audiences? Should the hero lead with impact or with services? |

## Option 2 — What-to-Why (Standard)

**Source text:**

> Terra Nexus is an innovation and insight studio that equips food and
> agribusinesses with the strategies, tools and supporting capabilities to
> launch commercial sustainability offerings. We integrate proven solutions
> that enhance transparency, facilitate collaboration, and reward performance
> at every node in the food value chain, because we believe that with the
> right partners, we can build a more resilient, sustainable and prosperous
> food system

| Attribute | Assessment [agent-draft] |
|---|---|
| **Intended use** | Homepage hero, elevator pitch, short About statement |
| **Strengths** | Leads with what Terra Nexus does; concise; clearly names the client ("food and agribusinesses") and outcome ("launch commercial sustainability offerings") |
| **Weaknesses** | "Launch commercial sustainability offerings" may read as implementation-only; does not reference climate explicitly |
| **Length** | Short (one paragraph) |
| **Primary audience** | Potential clients evaluating whether Terra Nexus is relevant to their business |
| **Likely placement** | Homepage hero, About lede, proposal cover |
| **Unresolved choices** | Is "launch" the right verb or should it be broader (advise, manage, operate)? Should "climate" appear in this version? |

| Attribute | Assessment [agent-draft] |

| **Strengths** | Adds "food and climate" explicitly; uses "profitable" which signals commercial orientation; covers transparency, collaboration, performance |

| **Primary audience** | Food and agribusiness leaders evaluating commercial sustainability programs |
| **Likely placement** | Homepage hero block or expanded About statement |
| **Unresolved choices** | Does "profitable" need qualification? Is "developing impact" the right verb phrase? |

## Option 4 — Combo-Hybrid (Standard)

**Source text:**

> Terra Nexus is an innovation and insights studio developing impact at the
> intersection of food & climate. We integrate proven solutions that enhance
> transparency, facilitate collaboration and reward positive performance for
> food & agribusinesses across the entire food system. Our team provides the
> insights, tools and supporting capabilities to help food and agribusinesses
> achieve commercial success on the path to a more resilient, sustainable and
> prosperous food system.

| Attribute | Assessment [agent-draft] |
|---|---|
| **Intended use** | Homepage hero or About page; most balanced option |
| **Strengths** | Two-sentence structure is scannable; "reward positive performance" names the mechanism; closes with "commercial success" which signals business orientation; ends with purpose |
| **Weaknesses** | Slightly redundant (food & agribusinesses appears twice); "Combo-Hybrid" construction may feel less distinctive than a single strong voice |
| **Length** | Medium (two short paragraphs) |
| **Primary audience** | Broadest — works for most visitor segments |
| **Likely placement** | Homepage hero, proposal summaries, pitch decks |
| **Unresolved choices** | Should "commercial success" and "resilient, sustainable and prosperous food system" both appear in the same sentence, or should they be separated? |

## Option 5 — Combo-Hybrid (Extended)

**Source text:**

> Terra Nexus is an innovation and insight studio developing impact at the
> intersection of food and climate. We advise, manage, and operate commercial
> sustainability programs that integrate fit-for-purpose solutions at every
> node in the food value chain, to enhance transparency, facilitate
> collaboration and establish the market mechanisms needed to build trust and
> reward performance across the entire food system. Our team provides the
> insights, tools and supporting capabilities to help food and agribusinesses
> achieve commercial success on the path to a more resilient, sustainable and
> prosperous food system

| Attribute | Assessment [agent-draft] |
|---|---|
| **Intended use** | Homepage hero for audiences already familiar with the food-and-climate advisory space; Advise/Manage/Operate-led positioning |
| **Strengths** | Only option that names Advise, Manage, Operate explicitly; "fit-for-purpose solutions" signals customization; "market mechanisms" signals environmental markets expertise |
| **Weaknesses** | Longest option; mentions three lines of business in a single sentence which may obscure any one of them; most complex for first-time visitors |
| **Length** | Long (two paragraphs) |
| **Primary audience** | Procurement, sustainability, and environmental markets professionals who already understand advisory models |
| **Likely placement** | Homepage (below the fold), extended About page, investor or partnership materials |
| **Unresolved choices** | Is the explicit naming of Advise/Manage/Operate helpful or confusing for general website visitors? Should this be the primary or secondary narrative? |

4. Should the three lines of business (Advise, Manage, Operate) appear in the hero?
