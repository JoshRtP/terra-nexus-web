# Advise

Terra Nexus serves as an advisor, helping clients evaluate decisions and apply the relevant service-line capabilities.

# Manage

Terra Nexus manages another organization's program or defined program workstreams as an operating partner.

# Operate

Terra Nexus develops and operates its own solutions or programs.

# Governing Rule

Advise, Manage, and Operate describe **how Terra Nexus engages**. They are not service lines and should not replace the five service families.
