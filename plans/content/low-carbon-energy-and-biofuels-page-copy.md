## Build More Valuable Biofuel Supply Chains with Verifiable Carbon Intensity

Low-carbon fuel value depends on more than producing a favorable
carbon-intensity score.

Biofuel producers and feedstock suppliers must determine where the value
originates, which feedstock can support it, what evidence is required, how
physical commodities and environmental attributes move through the supply
chain, how value is allocated, and whether the resulting calculations and
claims can withstand independent review.

A field-level result may begin with agronomic data, but commercial execution
reaches across producer enrollment, grain origination, lifecycle modeling,
contracts, inventory controls, tax and regulatory requirements, and
verification.

Terra Nexus helps clients connect those decisions into a program designed to
create value and operate under scrutiny.

## From the Field to the Fuel Pathway

| Program stage | Questions the program must answer |
|---|---|
| Field and producer | Which data and records support the crop's carbon intensity? |
| Feedstock analysis | How do location, yield, nutrient management, and eligible practices affect CI? |
| Origination | Where can reduced-CI volume be sourced, and how should it be priced? |
| Aggregation | How are eligible and conventional volumes controlled and reconciled? |
| Chain of custody | How are product characteristics transferred without unsupported claims or double counting? |
| Fuel pathway | How does feedstock CI interact with the applicable GREET pathway and facility emissions? |
| Commercial value | How are premiums, incentive value, costs, and risks allocated? |
| Program operations | Which calculations, ledgers, reviews, and exception controls must recur? |
| Verification | Can the evidence, transactions, calculations, and claims withstand independent review? |

## FD-CIC – Carbon Intensity Analysis Platform

Terra Nexus's **FD-CIC – Carbon Intensity Analysis Platform** is a productized
capability supported by proprietary accelerators and tools.

The platform extends USDA FD-CIC analysis into a broader field-to-fuel
commercial and operating workflow.

### Platform Capabilities

- field- and management-unit-level feedstock analysis;
- direct use and integration of USDA FD-CIC inputs and outputs;
- scenario comparison across location, yield, nutrients, and practices;
- connection to applicable GREET pathway analysis;
- facility and feedstock value modeling;
- partial-traceability and mixed-supply scenarios;
- producer, originator, and facility value-allocation modeling;
- source-data, unit, and assumption tracking;
- calculation versioning and reproducible outputs;
- audit-ready workpapers and review packages.

### Designed for Decisions, Not Just Calculations

The platform helps clients evaluate questions such as:

- Which portions of current feedstock supply offer the greatest potential value?
- How does incomplete traceability affect the business case?
- Which field-level inputs materially change feedstock CI?
- What premium can be paid while preserving facility or originator economics?
- How should value be shared among producers, traders, and refiners?
- Which records support each input, calculation, volume, and claim?
- What exceptions must be resolved before the program is verification-ready?

The platform does not replace USDA FD-CIC, DOE 45ZCF-GREET, tax advice, or
independent verification. It connects reference tools and source evidence to
the commercial and operational decisions clients must make.

## Diligence That Follows the Value to Its Source

Modeled value is only as reliable as the underlying feedstock, evidence,
calculations, controls, and representations.

Terra Nexus supports detailed field-to-fuel diligence, including:

- field-level source-document review;
- crop and feedstock eligibility assessment;
- yield, nutrient, and practice substantiation;
- calculation integrity and unit testing;
- source-data reconciliation;
- chain-of-custody and mass-balance review;
- physical-volume and environmental-attribute controls;
- contractual rights and representations;
- operational-control and segregation-of-duties review;
- anomaly, fraud, and duplicate-claim indicators;
- analysis of unsupported or non-verifiable claims;
- tax-credit transaction and investor diligence;
- verification and audit readiness.

Our approach evaluates both the projected value and the conditions required for
that value to be supportable.
