# Low Carbon Energy & Biofuels — Architecture Alignment Note

> Owner approved this document as a first-draft repository baseline on August 3, 2026.
> It is not public-production approval.

**Status:** Internal review note; does not amend the owner-approved brief.

## Position in the value chain

**Primary value-chain node:** Energy & Biofuels

Depends on upstream Agricultural Production and Commodity Trading and may create downstream regulatory, tax, fuel, customer, and environmental value.

## Role of the Area of Expertise

Focuses on changing feedstock, origination, processing, carbon-intensity, evidence, chain-of-custody, and operating systems to create supportable low-carbon fuel value.

## Relationship to Cultivation to Claim

Cultivation to Claim is not a page stage. A low-carbon biofuel play may begin
with changes at Agricultural Production, move through Commodity Trading and
chain-of-custody systems, and be commercialized at the Energy & Biofuels node
through regulatory, tax, fuel, customer, or environmental claims.

Terra Nexus evaluates the supportable pathways, evidence requirements,
participant-specific economics, commercial utilization, and stacking constraints
without disclosing its full intervention and commercialization playbooks on the
public page.

## Illustrative claim and value categories

- regulatory fuel and tax-credit value
- low-carbon feedstock qualification
- certification and chain-of-custody claims
- carbon-intensity and customer claims
- tradable environmental attributes where applicable
- corporate reporting and Scope 3 value
- margin, sourcing, and supply-security value
