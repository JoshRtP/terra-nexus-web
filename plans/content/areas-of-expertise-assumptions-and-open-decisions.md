# Areas of Expertise — Assumptions and Open Decisions Register

> Owner approved this document as a first-draft repository baseline on August 3, 2026.
> It is not public-production approval.

**Version:** 0.9

## Owner-confirmed decisions

1. Value-chain nodes define where activity occurs and align with Who We Work With.
2. Areas of Expertise define the domain in which change is pursued.
3. Interventions are fit-for-purpose changes and are not the public-page taxonomy.
4. Claim is an umbrella term covering financial and nonfinancial, public and internal,
   chain-bound and transferable forms of value.
5. Cultivation to Claim is an integration and commercialization framework, not a
   sequence of website stages.
6. A single play may combine several Areas of Expertise and value-chain nodes.
7. Terra Nexus may serve one client while optimizing the requirements and shared
   value across all participants required to deliver the play.
8. Highest value means the greatest realizable, risk-adjusted shared value—not the
   highest nominal price or lowest implementation cost.
9. Claim stacking, ownership, utilization, and double-counting are central client
   questions but do not need a standalone public framework page yet.
10. Detailed intervention libraries, pricing, stacking logic, and client playbooks
    should remain proprietary and outside the public website.

## Shared content assumptions retained

- Global relevance is the default; Low Carbon Energy & Biofuels remains U.S.-led.
- Advise and Manage are the standard public engagement models.
- Operate is omitted unless Terra Nexus owns and runs the program.
- Case studies and related insights are optional modules.
- No named clients or unapproved quantified outcomes are used.
- Current frameworks, programs, policies, and regulations require official-source
  packaging before publication.
- Public copy may be reviewed before research completion but remains blocked from production.
