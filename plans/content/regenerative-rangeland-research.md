## Purpose

This memo defines the research needed to support the Regenerative Rangeland Area of
Expertise page. It intentionally avoids presenting time-sensitive market,
regulatory, program, methodology, certification, or framework claims as
publication-ready facts.

## Working positioning to test

> Regenerative rangeland creates durable value when grazing management, herd performance, land condition, producer economics, measurement, commercial incentives, and downstream market requirements are designed as one operating system.

Research should test whether this thesis is relevant to the primary audience,
commercially differentiated, and supportable across the intended geographies.

## Priority research questions

- Current global and regional rangeland condition, resilience, and livestock-market drivers
- Current corporate beef, dairy, leather, and livestock Scope 3 requirements
- Current grazing, soil-carbon, biodiversity, and sustainable-beef frameworks
- Current MRV approaches for soil, vegetation, grazing, and livestock outcomes
- Current claims and certification rules affecting grassfed, regenerative, climate, and nature claims

## Candidate framework and mechanism set

- GHG Protocol land-sector and agricultural accounting
- SBTi FLAG
- ISO 14064
- Verra agricultural land-management methodologies
- BCarbon soil-carbon protocols
- Climate Action Reserve land-sector methodologies
- American Carbon Registry land methodologies
- Grassfed, animal-welfare, and sustainable-beef frameworks
- nature and biodiversity frameworks, including TNFD

Each item requires an official source record before publication containing:

- exact title;
- issuer or owner;
- current version or effective date;
- publication or revision date;
- canonical URL;
- access date;
- current status;
- specific claim supported;
- update sensitivity;
- geographic and participant applicability.

## Claims register

| Claim area | Required source type | Update sensitivity | Draft treatment |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Market size, growth, adoption, or investment | Government, multilateral, peer-reviewed, or recognized market data | High | Omit until sourced |
| Environmental or social outcomes | Peer-reviewed science or official technical guidance | High | Avoid universal claims |
| Corporate accounting or target requirements | Official framework owner | Very high | State version and date |
| Regulation, policy, funding, or eligibility | Government or regulator | Very high | Recheck before publication |
| Certification or methodology status | Scheme, registry, or standard owner | Very high | Do not infer active eligibility |
| Technology performance | Technical documentation plus independent evidence where possible | High | Treat vendor claims as self-reported |
| Commercial premiums or ROI | Approved Terra Nexus proof or reputable market evidence | High | Do not generalize |
| Terra Nexus experience | Owner confirmation and approved proof boundaries | Per engagement | General only unless governed proof exists |

## Peer-positioning research

Review representative organizations across:

- strategy and advisory firms;
- producers, traders, processors, brands, and retailers;
- technology and MRV providers;
- standards, certifications, registries, and assurance providers;
- project and program operators;
- investors and market infrastructure.

Peer research should inform differentiation only. Do not reuse peer language
or treat self-reported outcomes as independent evidence.

## Terra Nexus differentiation to test

- integration across commercial, operational, technical, environmental, and assurance decisions;
- food and agricultural value-chain knowledge;
- commodity, sourcing, and operating-model fluency;
- independent technology, methodology, and partner diligence;
- economics, value allocation, and commercialization;
- traceability, controls, and claims readiness;
- Advise and embedded Manage delivery models.

## Guardrails

- Do not present illustrative organizations as clients, partners, or completed engagements.
- Do not guarantee environmental, financial, certification, regulatory, or market outcomes.
- Do not imply that Terra Nexus performs accredited validation or verification.
- Do not provide legal, tax, accounting, investment, or certification opinions.
- Use qualified agronomic, ecological, engineering, animal-health, food-safety, or technical specialists where the work requires licensed or specialized expertise.
- Case studies and qualifications are optional modular records; omit empty proof sections rather than display placeholders.
- Current standards, programs, regulations, and market facts require official-source verification before public production.
- Advise and Manage are the default public engagement models. Operate is omitted unless Terra Nexus owns and runs a program under its own name.
