## Why this exists

The family page carries a one-line label per offering and a list of
sub-offering labels averaging eleven words. Peers describe four to six
services per page at a paragraph each. The Carbon & Ecosystem Services
page now does the same from its approved offering records, and this
worksheet collects the equivalent for this family. Nothing here is
published until you have filled it in and said so; agents must not invent
capability claims (AGENTS.md rule 9).

## How to fill it in

For each offering, answer the five prompts in plain sentences. Bullet
fragments are fine; the page renders them as lists. Aim for 120 to 200
words per offering. Leave a prompt blank if the honest answer is "we do
not do that", and say so in the pruning section. The answers go into
`offerings[].detail` in the data module, the same shape C&ES uses, and
into a new offering record in `knowledge/services/` for governance.

The five prompts:

1. **Who decides.** Which role in the client organisation owns this decision or buys this work?
2. **The question they bring.** In their words, what are they trying to answer? (Rendered as a quotation.)
3. **Typical situations.** Three or four problems that bring a client to this offering. Real patterns, not named clients.
4. **What they receive.** The deliverables: memo, model, plan, framework, workshop, dashboard.
5. **When it is this and not a neighbour.** One sentence on the boundary with the adjacent offering or family.
