## From Improved Performance to the Right Market Value

Improving performance does not automatically create value.

The same operational or environmental improvement may support participation in
a program, a certification, a product claim, a Scope 3 reduction, an inset, a
tradable environmental asset, a regulatory incentive, corporate reporting, or
greater resilience and supply security.

Terra Nexus helps clients connect fit-for-purpose changes with the evidence,
collaboration, operating model, and market mechanisms required to create the
highest-value supportable outcome across the participants needed to deliver it.

### Transparency

Clarify the performance, evidence, costs, ownership, risks, and value available
to each participant.

### Collaboration

Align the producers, suppliers, traders, processors, brands, retailers,
technology providers, investors, and market partners required to make the
solution work.

### Market Mechanisms

Design incentives, commercial structures, claims, attributes, and routes to
market that reward the participants whose actions create the value.
