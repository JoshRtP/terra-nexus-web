**Status:** Owner-directed first-refresh architecture  
**Publication:** This contract governs protected-preview implementation; it does not approve draft copy for production.

# First Refresh Public Page Contract

## Governing objective

Build the first refresh from the top down so that a homepage visitor can quickly
understand:

1. what Terra Nexus is known for;
2. the capabilities Terra Nexus brings;
3. who Terra Nexus works with to create value; and
4. evidence of impact.

The governed repository may remain deeper than the public website.

## Public terminology

| Internal taxonomy | Public label |
|---|---|
| Area of Expertise | Expertise / Areas of Expertise |
| Service Family | Capability |
| Service Offering | Offering module |

| Case Study / Qualification | Case Study / Proof |

## First-refresh page hierarchy

### Homepage

`/`

Top-level orientation, brand, expertise, capabilities, audiences, shared-value
approach, engagement models, proof, and CTA.

### Expertise

- `/expertise/`
- nine dedicated topic pages under `/expertise/<slug>/`

Every Area of Expertise receives a dedicated page.

Do not create mandatory pages below the topic level.

### Capabilities

- `/capabilities/`
- five dedicated capability pages under `/capabilities/<slug>/`

The 29 Service Offerings are embedded modules within the five capability pages.

Do not create standalone routes for the 29 offerings in the first refresh.

The deeper sub-offerings remain internal capability detail and may be used
selectively as examples, representative activities, engagement language, tags,
or future page inputs.

### Who We Work With

- `/who-we-work-with/`
- `/who-we-work-with/food-and-agribusiness-value-chain/`
- `/who-we-work-with/enabling-markets-technology-and-capital/`

The landing page uses the approved value-chain-with-enablers diagram.

The eight physical value-chain segments and three enabler segments are modules
or anchored sections within the two group pages.

Do not create 11 individual audience-segment pages in the first refresh.

### Case Studies

- `/case-studies/`
- one detail route for each approved case-study record

Case studies remain reusable governed records connected to capabilities,
expertise, audiences, engagement models, and impacts.

### Supporting pages

- `/about/`
- `/contact/`

### Engagement-model treatment

Advise and Manage are integrated into the homepage, capability pages, and other
relevant page sections. They do not receive standalone routes in the first
refresh.

Operate is separate from the general client engagement model. It may be
presented only for a specific Terra Nexus-owned solution or program that has
received public-content approval. Do not create a general Operate page or present
Operate as a standard client service.

Insights is deferred from primary navigation until an approved minimum content
library exists.

## Page-component rules

### Offering modules

Each of the 29 offering modules may include:

- name;
- concise description;
- client decision or problem;
- three to six representative activities;
- optional related expertise, audience, or proof;
- optional anchor.

No standalone hero, SEO page, research memo, or independent CTA is required.

### Audience modules

Each audience module may include:

- segment name and role in the system;
- decisions or value Terra Nexus helps address;
- relevant expertise;
- relevant capabilities;
- optional proof.

### Proof modules

Proof is optional on expertise, capability, and audience pages. Omit the module
when no relevant approved record exists.

## Navigation recommendation

- Who We Work With
- Case Studies
- About
- Contact

## Deferred or excluded from the first refresh

- standalone pages for 29 offerings;
- standalone pages for approximately 110 sub-offerings;
- standalone pages for 11 audience segments;
- standalone pages for Advise, Manage, or Operate;
- an Insights section without approved content;
- a standalone Cultivation-to-Claim page;
- detailed intervention libraries;
- claim-stacking decision trees;
- pricing databases and optimization models;
- client-specific playbooks;
- unapproved biographies, qualifications, claims, or proof.

## Codex implementation boundary

The next implementation phase should consume the approved page copy and governed
records without expanding the page inventory or promoting lower-level taxonomy
records into routes unless the owner changes this decision.
