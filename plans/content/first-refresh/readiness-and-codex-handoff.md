## Engagement-model decision

- **Advise** and **Manage** are the public client engagement models.
- They are integrated into relevant pages and do not receive standalone routes
  in the first refresh.
- **Operate** is separate and applies only to selected Terra Nexus-owned
  solutions or programs.
- Do not present Operate as a general client service or create a generic Operate
  page.

## Content-level decisions

- Publicly use **Capabilities** for the five governed Service Families.
- The 29 Service Offerings are embedded modules, not standalone routes.
- Approximately 110 sub-offerings remain internal capability detail.
- All nine Areas of Expertise receive dedicated pages.
- Who We Work With uses one diagram-led landing page and two group pages.
- The eight physical value-chain segments and three enabler segments are
  embedded modules, not standalone routes.
- Case studies remain reusable governed proof records.
- Insights is deferred from primary navigation until an approved minimum
  library exists.
- Cultivation to Claim remains a company-level framework, not a required
  standalone page in this release.

## Codex implementation boundary

Codex should build only the page inventory defined in
`plans/content/first-refresh/public-page-contract.md`.

It should not:

- create standalone offering, audience-segment, or engagement-model routes;
- expose blocked research or proprietary intervention content;
- create new case-study claims;
- invent team biographies, contact details, client names, outcomes,
  certifications, or qualifications;
- promote draft copy to public production; or
- add Insights as a primary section without approved content.
