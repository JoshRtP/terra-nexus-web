## Current Market and Regulatory Research

### Section 45Z

The working content package records that the Section 45Z Clean Fuel Production
Credit is available for qualified clean transportation fuel produced
domestically and sold through December 31, 2029; that the claimant must be
registered as a clean-fuel producer at the time of production; and that
feedstock-origin requirements apply for fuel produced after December 31, 2025.

The package also records that Treasury and IRS issued proposed Section 45Z
regulations in February 2026 and that those rules remained proposed as of the
research date. These statements require a final primary-source citation and
freshness check before publication.

### 45ZCF-GREET

The package records that DOE identifies 45ZCF-GREET as the model adopted by
Treasury for Section 45Z emissions-rate determinations and distinguishes
regulatory GREET models from R&D GREET. Model-version statements must be tied to
the applicable DOE release before publication.

### USDA FD-CIC and Feedstock Rule

The package records that USDA published the final rule, **Technical Guidelines
for the Production of Regenerative Agricultural Biofuel Feedstocks**, on June
29, 2026, with an effective date of July 29, 2026.

The package identifies USDA FD-CIC coverage for:

- field corn;
- soybeans;
- sorghum;
- spring canola.

It also identifies nutrient management and specified practices including:

- no-till;
- reduced till;
- cover crops;
- nitrification inhibitors;
- manure nitrogen.

The package states that the rule addresses field- or management-unit-level
quantification, reporting, chain of custody, recordkeeping, mass balance, and
verification, and that feedstock information must pass through the supply chain
to the biofuel refiner. Each statement requires direct citation to the final
rule and current USDA tool documentation before publication.

## Peer-Page Review

The working peer set clusters into five common approaches:

1. farmer and agronomy platforms emphasizing field data, producer enrollment,
   CI scoring, and farmer monetization;
2. operational agriculture advisers emphasizing farms, elevators, co-ops,
   ethanol facilities, decision tools, and audit-ready records;
3. fuel and commodity intelligence firms emphasizing market data, feedstock
   economics, policy, and investment planning;
4. LCA, compliance, and verification specialists emphasizing CI modeling,
   certification, validation, and verification;
5. tax and accounting firms emphasizing eligibility, registration, filing,
   transferability, and tax risk.

## Positioning Guardrails

Do not claim that Terra Nexus:

- provides tax opinions;
- guarantees credit eligibility or value;
- performs accredited verification;
- certifies fuel or feedstock;
- replaces USDA FD-CIC or DOE 45ZCF-GREET;
- guarantees that a modeled CI result will be accepted;
- represents illustrative companies as clients.
