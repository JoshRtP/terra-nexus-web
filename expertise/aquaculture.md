# Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Aquaculture covers the farming of fish, shellfish, seaweed, and other aquatic organisms for food, feed, and other uses. It encompasses freshwater and marine systems, pond aquaculture, cage culture, recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS), and seaweed cultivation.

# Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Aquaculture is the fastest-growing food production sector globally and is increasingly scrutinized for its feed sourcing, land-use footprint, water quality impacts, and carbon emissions. Food companies with seafood supply chains face growing certification, disclosure, and sourcing requirements.
