# Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Sustainable Supply Chains covers the strategies, programs, tools, and collaborations that reduce environmental impact, cost, and risk across the food and agribusiness value chain — from farm-level sourcing through processing, logistics, and retail.

# Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Scope 3 Category 1 (purchased goods and services) is typically the largest GHG category for food companies. Pressure from corporate net-zero targets, investor ESG requirements, customer demands, and regulatory disclosure mandates (CSRD, SEC, TCFD) is accelerating supply chain sustainability programs across the value chain.
