# Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Food Waste (Prevention, Diversion, Recovery) addresses strategies, programs, and business models for reducing food loss and waste across the supply chain — through prevention at source, surplus food recovery and donation, composting and organics recycling, anaerobic digestion, and other circular approaches.

# Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Food waste is responsible for a significant share of global GHG emissions and represents substantial value destruction across the supply chain. Corporate Scope 3 targets, food waste disclosure requirements, and growing regulatory mandates in the US, EU, and elsewhere are driving food companies to measure, report, and reduce food waste.
