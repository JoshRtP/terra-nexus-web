# Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Agroforestry integrates trees and shrubs into crop and livestock systems to deliver food production alongside ecological outcomes — including carbon sequestration, biodiversity improvement, soil health, and watershed protection.

# Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Agroforestry is increasingly relevant to food companies seeking supply chain sustainability outcomes beyond row-crop carbon programs. Trees in farming systems offer longer-duration carbon removals and biodiversity co-benefits. High-value commodities including cocoa, coffee, palm, and specialty crops are primary agroforestry contexts.
