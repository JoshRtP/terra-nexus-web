# Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Biodiversity & Ecosystem Resilience addresses how food and agribusiness operations affect — and can restore — native habitats, species diversity, ecosystem function, and the long-term resilience of natural systems that underpin food production.

# Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Biodiversity loss is increasingly recognized as a material financial and operational risk for food companies. TNFD disclosure frameworks, CSRD biodiversity requirements, and growing investor focus on nature-related risks are creating new reporting and strategy obligations for food and agribusiness.
