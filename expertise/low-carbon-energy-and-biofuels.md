# Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Low Carbon Energy & Biofuels covers the production, sustainability, and commercial positioning of ethanol, renewable diesel, biodiesel, sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), renewable natural gas (RNG), and other low-carbon fuels derived from agricultural feedstocks.

# Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Agricultural feedstocks are central to the US and global low-carbon fuel supply. Regulatory programs including the US Renewable Fuel Standard, California LCFS, and the 45Z Clean Fuel Production Credit create significant commercial and compliance incentives for feedstock sustainability. Feedstock traceability, GHG intensity verification, and sustainability certification are increasingly required by regulators and fuel buyers.
