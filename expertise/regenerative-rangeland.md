# Topic Definition [agent-draft]

Regenerative Rangeland covers the management of grasslands, pastures, and grazing systems to improve soil health, carbon sequestration, biodiversity, water cycling, and long-term productivity — while supporting the livelihoods of livestock producers.

# Why the Topic Matters [agent-draft — external facts require current research]

Grasslands and rangelands represent some of the largest terrestrial carbon stores globally. Livestock supply chains are under increasing Scope 3 scrutiny from food companies with net-zero targets. Regenerative rangeland management offers pathways to carbon credits, biodiversity outcomes, and supply chain sustainability claims.
