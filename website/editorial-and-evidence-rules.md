# Source Discipline

* Use approved source content and stable concepts before drafting new claims.
* Distinguish source-derived content from new market research or copywriting.
* Cite current external sources for time-sensitive regulations, programs, standards, market facts, and company claims.

# Client and Company Examples

* Companies listed in audience concepts are illustrative examples.
* Do not call an example company a client, partner, investor, verifier, or provider used by Terra Nexus unless an approved case study or qualification explicitly says so.

# Case Studies

* Do not invent client names, outcomes, metrics, geographies, standards, or deliverables.
* Use anonymized case studies when required.
* Separate one client's multiple engagements into reusable qualification modules when appropriate, but do not imply multiple clients.

# Brand

* Preserve approved purpose, mission, positioning, and terminology.
* Do not collapse Advise, Manage, and Operate into the service taxonomy.
* Do not describe Terra Nexus as a generic consulting firm.
