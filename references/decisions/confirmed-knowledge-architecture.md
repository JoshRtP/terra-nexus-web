# Confirmed Knowledge Architecture

The website knowledge system uses five distinct dimensions. They must not be collapsed into one another.

| Dimension | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Service Lines** | Organize capabilities by the client function or decision owner buying the work. |
| **Areas of Expertise** | Define the food-and-climate topics that create market relevance, urgency, and calls to action. |
| **Who We Work With** | Define the value-chain and enabling-market audiences Terra Nexus serves. |
| **Advise, Manage, Operate** | Define Terra Nexus's three lines of business and the role Terra Nexus plays in an engagement. |
| **Case Studies and Qualifications** | Provide evidence, tagged across the other dimensions. |

# Service-Line Interpretation

A capability may appear in more than one service line when different client functions own different decisions. Do not deduplicate or relocate an offering solely because similar work appears elsewhere.

The determining question is:

> Which client function owns the decision, budget, accountability, and desired outcome?

# Website Interpretation

* Topic pages should function as market-facing entry points and calls to action.
* Service pages should explain the decision area, primary buyer, and capabilities.
* Audience pages should explain the role of each value-chain group and connect it to relevant topics and services.
* Advise, Manage, and Operate should be described separately from the service taxonomy.
* Case studies should be reusable proof modules rather than isolated stories.
