# Terra Nexus Service Lines

Service lines are organized by client function and decision owner.

# Service Families

* [Strategy & Innovation](strategy-and-innovation/) - Where should we play and how do we win at the intersection of food and climate?
* [Financial Investments & New Venture Development](financial-investments-and-new-venture-development/) - Which food-and-climate ideas, ventures, investments, assets, partnerships, or facilities should be screened, funded, incubated, acquired, or scaled?
* [Sustainable Supply Chain & Operations](sustainable-supply-chain-and-operations/) - How can impacts, risk, cost, and volatility be reduced by changing what is bought, who it is bought from, how it is produced, moved, processed, used, or recovered?
* [Corporate Sustainability](corporate-sustainability/) - How should the enterprise set targets and policies, prioritize action, account for progress, manage claims, and report performance?
* [Carbon & Ecosystem Services](carbon-and-ecosystem-services/) - How are environmental assets, attributes, credits, insets, offsets, and claims created, accessed, valued, verified, accounted for, managed, and monetized?
