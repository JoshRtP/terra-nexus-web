# Purpose

How should the enterprise set targets and policies, prioritize action, account
for progress, manage claims, and report performance?

# Primary Decision Owners

* Chief Sustainability Officers (CSOs)
* ESG and climate teams
* Compliance and risk leaders
* Reporting and disclosure teams
* Legal and enterprise governance

# Core Decision

How does the enterprise define its sustainability ambition, set goals and policies,
measure and account for progress, manage Scope 1/2/3 strategy, govern claims,
and report performance to stakeholders?

# Food x Climate Scope Boundary

This service family applies to enterprise sustainability strategy, Scope 1/2/3
decarbonization, accounting, reporting, disclosure, governance, and stakeholder
engagement.

**What belongs here:**
- Corporate sustainability strategy, materiality, and ambition-setting
- Scope 1, 2, and 3 strategy, accounting, and target-setting
- ESG, TCFD, CSRD, CDP, SBTi, and regulatory disclosure management
- Claims governance and greenwashing risk management
- Internal and external stakeholder engagement
- ESG operating model and governance design

**What does not belong here:**
- Environmental asset or credit market participation (see Carbon & Ecosystem Services)
- Operational supply-chain changes when owned by procurement/operations (see SSC&O)
- Corporate strategy and market positioning (see Strategy & Innovation)

**Note on intentional overlap with SSC&O:** This service family contains a supply
chain and operations block because CSOs frequently sponsor supply-chain
sustainability work through the enterprise program framework. The same activities
may appear in SSC&O when sponsored by procurement/operations. This overlap is
confirmed as intentional in the approved service architecture.

**Note on intentional boundary with Carbon & Ecosystem Services:** Corporate
Sustainability is not normally the commercial owner of environmental assets,
credit transactions, or customer-facing environmental market programs. When the
environmental markets team or a trade desk is the decision owner, the work sits
in Carbon & Ecosystem Services.
