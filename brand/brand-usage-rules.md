# Required Framing

* Describe Terra Nexus as an **innovation and insights studio**.
* Keep the focus at the intersection of **food and climate**.
* Preserve the three lines of business: **Advise, Manage, Operate**.
* Connect work to commercial sustainability and climate programs, fit-for-purpose solutions, transparency, collaboration, market mechanisms, trust, and rewarded performance.
* Use "food and agribusinesses" or the more specific audience segment when possible.

# Do Not

* Recast Terra Nexus as a generic management consulting, ESG consulting, software, carbon-credit, or project-development firm.
* Treat the example companies in audience documents as confirmed clients.
* Select one of the alternative narrative options as the final homepage hero without approval.
* Replace the approved purpose, mission, or positioning with newly invented copy.
