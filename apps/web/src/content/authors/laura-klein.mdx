Laura specializes in regenerative agriculture, applying sustainability and resiliency frameworks to a global scale-up of ecosystem services and nature-based decarbonization. Her work spans GHG accounting, program design and operations, voluntary carbon markets, and certification and assurance. She participates in industry working groups including Verra and the Global Roundtable for Sustainable Beef. Off the clock, she ski patrols at Eldora Mountain Resort in Boulder and trail runs with her two huskies, Maize and Fitz.
